Calculation and pre-departure checks

Round-trip travel = 2 × planar route meters ÷ (assumed speed × 1,000) × 60. Margin = available minutes − task minutes − reserve minutes − travel minutes.

Constant speed and the same outward/return path are assumed. This is a planning estimate, not a live countdown. Slopes, obstacles, delays and real surface distance are unknown.

  • Independently verify suit/life support, supplies and operating limits.
  • Establish communications and an accessible return point.
  • Review terrain and hazards using validated operational sources.
  • Save the route and observations, export the mission, and verify the offline pack before disconnecting.

Historical REMS observations and AI-generated relief cannot establish current conditions or real route slope. Scenario assumptions are stored separately from route/notes; the text brief is not an importable mission JSON.
