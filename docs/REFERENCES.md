2. **NASA Science — Mars Science Laboratory (Curiosity)** — Mission overview and Mount Sharp findings: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/ (Credit: NASA / JPL-Caltech)
3. **NASA Science — Curiosity's Proposed Path Up Mount Sharp** — Traverse context: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/curiositys-proposed-path-up-mount-sharp/ (Credit: NASA / JPL-Caltech / MSSS)
4. **NASA Humans to Mars** — Exploration context: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/humans-to-mars/ (Credit: NASA)
5. **NASA Mars 2020 Raw Images** — Raw image archive reference: https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/multimedia/raw-images/ (Credit: NASA / JPL-Caltech)
6. **NASA Worldview** — Earthdata visualization reference: https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/ (Credit: NASA EOSDIS)
7. **USGS Geologic Map of Mars (SIM-3292)** — Tanaka et al. (2014), 1:20,000,000 scale: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov (Credit: U.S. Geological Survey)
